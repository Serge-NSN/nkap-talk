NkapTalk settings

Pidgin voice assistant for digital banking.

Gemini (optional)

Without a key, NkapTalk still works: the page is translated from its built-in Pidgin phrasebook, and you can talk with the browser's speech recognition or type. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Gemini's voice receives the reply text, which includes values such as amounts. Leave it off when the bank's rules forbid sending displayed data.

Page language

The page can show Pidgin or a Cameroonian language. NkapTalk always talks in Pidgin, so the voice stays clear and every amount is read correctly.
