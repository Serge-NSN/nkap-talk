# Recording script — NkapTalk Bulu voice

Generated by `node tools/build-clips.js script bum`. Do not edit by hand.

NkapTalk writes its replies in Pidgin and plays your Bulu recording in their place, so the
lines below are the Pidgin ones. Say each in Bulu. Nothing you record is translated by a
machine afterwards, and no number you say is reused anywhere else.

**How to record.** One file per line below, named with the id in `code`. Any of `.webm`, `.ogg`, `.mp3`, `.m4a`, `.wav`, `.flac` works. Put them all in `nkaptalk-extension/audio/bum/`, then run `node tools/build-clips.js index bum`.

**Voice.** One speaker throughout, same room, same distance, no music. Trim the silence at both ends to about 50 ms: the clips are played back to back, so long silences make NkapTalk sound slow.

Do not read out any number that is not in Tier 1. Amounts always come from the page.

**Bulu is a tonal language.** Tone shifts with the words around it, so a sentence cut into
words and glued back together can say something nobody said. NkapTalk therefore plays each Tier 2 and
Tier 3 line as one whole recording and never cuts into it. Say each of those lines in one go.

This also keeps tone out of amounts: French has no tone, so an amount spliced into one of your sentences can never come out as a different word.
